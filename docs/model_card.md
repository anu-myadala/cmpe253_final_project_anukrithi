# Model card - mortgage-approval models used in the CMPE 253 fair-lending model-risk pipeline

_Generated by `src/make_docs.py` from `config.yaml` and `results/` on 2026-11-22; the structure follows Mitchell et al. (2019). Numbers are the pipeline's; the judgement fields are the author's._

## Model details
- Two baselines trained on the same inputs and split: scikit-learn logistic regression (C = 1.0) and LightGBM (400 trees, learning rate 0.05, 63 leaves).
- Inputs (12): numeric loan_amount, income, combined_loan_to_value_ratio, property_value, loan_term; categorical debt_to_income_ratio, applicant_credit_score_type, co-applicant_credit_score_type, aus-1, submission_of_application, conforming_loan_limit, co_applicant_present. Sensitive attributes (derived_race, derived_ethnicity, derived_sex, applicant_age) are never inputs. Census-tract proxies are off except in the proxy ablation.
- Preprocessing: median imputation + standardisation (numeric), one-hot with minimum frequency 20 (categorical). Seed 42. Versions: python 3.13.17, pandas 3.0.2, numpy 2.4.4, scikit-learn 1.8.0, lightgbm 4.7.0.
- Author: Anukrithi Myadala (SJSU, CMPE 253, Fall 2026). Research/teaching artefact, not a production model.

## Intended use
- Primary use: a measurement test-bed for model-risk evaluation (fairness, privacy, explainability, robustness) on public data.
- Out of scope: any real credit decision. The label is the lender's historical decision, not creditworthiness, so the models reproduce historical practice.

## Metrics
- Overall: AUC, accuracy, balanced accuracy, Brier score at a threshold chosen on the validation split (match_base_rate).
- Per group: selection rate, TPR, FPR, precision; gaps vs the reference with bootstrap 95 % CIs (B = 1000) and two-proportion z-tests; adverse-impact ratio; power note.

## Training and evaluation data
- HMDA public LAR, California, 2024: 297,908 downloaded rows -> 174,655 in the cohort (conventional, home purchase, originated or denied, owner-occupied, first lien, site-built, 1-4 units); approval rate 91.4 %.
- Split: train 104,793 / validation 34,931 / test 34,931 (stratified). Drift year 2025: 165,198 rows, never used for training.
- Leakage controls: post-decision fields dropped by keyword list plus an automated audit of every input category; the audit caught two structural leaks that the keyword list missed:
- `initially_payable_to_institution` = 3: 3,558 rows, approval rate 0 % (audit-only (dropped))
- `preapproval` = 1: 12,863 rows, approval rate 100 % (audit-only (dropped))
